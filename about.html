Working together

Clear thinking.
Open conversations.

You should understand the plan, know what needs your input, and be able to ask why. These are the principles I bring to the work.

  1. Understand before prescribing.

    Start with the business, the customer and the real constraint. The best next step depends on that context.

  2. Make priorities visible.

    Explain what matters, why it matters and what can wait. Keep the assumptions and trade-offs open to discussion.

  3. Stay close to what ships.

    Turn the plan into practical work. Review what changed and adjust when the evidence gives us a reason to.
