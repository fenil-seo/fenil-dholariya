Before we begin

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I hire you for just one service?+

Yes. We can scope one service or combine services around a business objective. The work should match the problem, not a fixed package.

How is a project priced?+

Full strategy and implementation engagements start at INR 1,000,000 (₹10 lakh). Individual services are scoped separately. Fees depend on complexity and delivery responsibilities. Share your budget and currency so we can discuss the fit.

How do we work across time zones?+

I am based in India, on IST (UTC+5:30). We agree meeting overlap, review points and written updates before starting. Include your location and preferred working hours in your enquiry.

Can you guarantee rankings or revenue?+

No. Results depend on your market, competition and implementation. We agree a baseline and measurement approach so you can assess progress.
