What does an engagement cost?+

Strategy and implementation engagements are typically scoped from ₹10 lakh. The final proposal depends on the problem, scope and delivery responsibilities.

What do you need from my team?+

Access to relevant search and analytics data, a decision maker, and someone who can help approve and implement changes.

No. Search performance depends on the market, competition and implementation. We agree a baseline, measurement window and responsibilities so progress can be assessed honestly.
